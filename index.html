MUS 601 · Fall 2026

Analysis of Musical Styles

Birth and death of tonality

A graduate research-and-analysis seminar at California State University, East Bay, tracing tonality from the Renaissance to the Romantic: modal practice moving toward functional harmony, the consolidation of common-practice tonality, and its chromatic saturation and dissolution across the nineteenth century. The real subject is becoming a scholar of this repertoire.

Tuesdays, 7:00–9:00 pm, Music Resource Center MB 1560, August 18 to December 1.
